# Smart City Energy Optimization for EVs and Renewable Integration

## Overview

This project was carried out during a summer internship at the LaRINa research laboratory in 2026. It focuses on intelligent energy management in smart cities through the integration of electric vehicles (EVs) and renewable energy sources.

The project uses **CityLearn**, an open-source energy simulation platform, to analyze energy consumption and evaluate reinforcement learning strategies for improving energy management.

## Objectives

* Explore and analyze energy consumption and photovoltaic (PV) production data.
* Implement a baseline control strategy using Basic Rule-Based Control (BasicRBC).
* Apply Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) reinforcement learning to energy management.
* Compare different reward functions and control strategies.
* Evaluate the trade-off between energy consumption, electricity cost, carbon emissions, and occupant comfort.

## Technologies and Tools

* **Programming:** Python
* **Simulation:** CityLearn 2.5.0
* **Reinforcement Learning:** Soft Actor-Critic (SAC)
* **Data Analysis:** NumPy, Matplotlib
* **Control Strategies:** BasicRBC, SAC-based strategies
* **Energy Systems:** Smart grids, photovoltaic energy, electric vehicles, and building energy management

## Methodology

1. Explore the CityLearn simulation environment and its energy datasets.
2. Analyze energy consumption and photovoltaic production across different time horizons.
3. Establish a baseline using rule-based control.
4. Implement and evaluate SAC-based strategies with different reward functions.
5. Compare the simulation results to assess energy efficiency and comfort.

## Key Results

The simulations showed that SAC-based strategies can improve energy-related performance compared with the BasicRBC baseline.

The results also highlighted the importance of selecting an appropriate reward function to balance energy consumption, electricity cost, carbon emissions, and occupant comfort.

The best strategy depends on the optimization objective and the acceptable comfort trade-off.
